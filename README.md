# Opinionated Guide to AI
By [Steven-Shine Chen](https://stevenshinechen.github.io), Twitter: [@stevenshinechen](https://x.com/stevenshinechen)

An opinionated collection of learning resources for AI. Covers the whole breadth from courses, to books, to podcasts and more. Includes everything you need, from learning how to code well to foundational mathematics behind AI.

These are the resources I've personally found useful and of high value. It is not a comprehensive list.

## Books

### Introductory
- [Neural Networks and Deep Learning](http://neuralnetworksanddeeplearning.com) (Michael Nielsen)
- [The Welch Labs Illustrated Guide to AI](https://www.welchlabs.com/store/illustrated-guide-to-ai) (Stephen Welch)

### Mathematics Background
- [Mathematics for Machine Learning](https://mml-book.github.io) (Marc Peter Deisenroth, A. Aldo Faisal, and Cheng Soon Ong)

### Reinforcement Learning
- [Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction](http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html) (Richard Sutton, Andrew Barto)
- [Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning:
Foundations and Modern Approaches](https://www.marl-book.com) (Stefano V. Albrecht,  Filippos Christianos, Lukas Schäfer)

### Information Theory
- Elements of Information Theory (Joy A. Thomas and Thomas M. Cover)

### Optimization
- Numerical Optimization (Jorge Nocedal and Stephen J. Wright)
